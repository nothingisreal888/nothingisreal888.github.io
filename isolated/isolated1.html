Isolated - Chapter 1

Falling. Everything, slipping away from my fingertips. Sunlight. The faint rays of sun being the last thing I saw before my world turned dark. \n Rays of sunlight. Shining specks of dust gently floating in the soft rays of the morning sun. A seemingly ordinary morning, yet it turned out to be a day I'll never forget. \n I wake up to the warm rays of sunlight and stare at the ceiling, waiting for me eyes to focus. Gathering my thoughts, I yawn and stretch before hopping out of the hammock I had made several weeks back with fabric I repurposed from tents in camspites which were long abandoned. I glance out of the window, and my gaze lands upon a section of the old cement structure -the skeletal remains of what once used to be a high school- across from the section that I lived in. "Home, sweet home..." I say to myself with a slightly amused grin. Although it was not at all a conventional living space, it was the best I could get while being able to live freely. I then climb the stairs up to the roof, which were located right outside of the classroom where I lived, and open the door to be met with a harsh gust of wind and a spray of sand in my face. "Ughhh..." I groan, spitting out sand from my mouth. Walking over to the rain collecter I had set up, I find that there was nearly nothing. "All right..." I go back inside the stairwell and record the amount that collected on the calendar. "About three more weeks until the next rainstorm," I calculate using the weather pattern data I gathered from the last two years. A rainstorm in this harsh climate is really rare, because of the Ocean Drainage Crisis just several years before this. Nearly the entire year goes on without a good rainstorm, apart from very small amounts of precipitation overnight about once every week and a half. However, there is a large rainstorm lasting a full day, but only about once a year. The pattern I found was that rain drastically decreases before these large rainstorms, similar to how water on a shore recedes right before a tsunami hits. "I guess I'll go pay a visit to the Black Market before the rain hits," I decide after thinking for a bit. The Black Market is actually more of a flea market and wanderer meetup rather than an illegal trade market, and it was unofficially named the 'Black Market' by visitors because it is only held during the night and completely painted black to avoid being detected by Aethel military scouting units. "I wonder if I'll bump into Kento there," I laugh at the thought, as Kento is my good friend and weather research partner. Thanks to our combined discoveries, many wanderers who visited the Black Market now know when rainstorms happen, and are able to prepare in advance for them. This also meant that the Black Market was going to be bustling, so I would have to head out early to stock up on supplies. "I guess I'll go get my bike out," I say as I stretch out in the soft, warm sunlight, and then put the calendar away into a desk just in case anybody else comes by the abandoned school and peeks inside. I then head out and towards the stairs to the first floor, right down the hall. "Ah... mornings are just the best," I say to myself while walking down the hall as a cool breeze floats through some empty window frames in nearby rooms. I walk down the sand-covered stairs to the first floor, where I go into an ordinary-looking janitor closet. There, I take a small screwdriver from one of the shelves and start unscrewing a hardly noticeable panel on the wall, which leads to a hidden storage room--or crawl space--which was directly underneath the stairwell. I ducked inside and turned on a light switch, illuminating the room with a small light bulb, which I had hooked up to a small wind-turbine on the roof, which was hidden away in an air vent so that the Aethel military units using helicopters would not see anything out of the ordinary when they do their occasional air patrol sweep. I walked over to an fairly large object covered by a tarp and took the tarp right off, revealing my prized dirt bike, being the exact one I got several years prior, before the incident.
